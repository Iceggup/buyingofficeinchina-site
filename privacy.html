Privacy

What happens to the information you send

Short version: it is used to answer your inquiry and follow up on your order. Nothing else.

What is collected

Only what you type into the inquiry form: your company, name, email, WhatsApp number, country, and the product details you send. The server also records the time of submission and a technical request identifier, used to limit spam.

What it is used for

  • Answering your inquiry and preparing a factory shortlist and quotation comparison for you
  • Following up on an order, if you place one
  • Nothing else

What it is not used for

  • No marketing list, no newsletters, no promotional email
  • Your details are not shared with any factory without your consent — factories receive your specification, not your identity, unless you ask me to introduce you
  • Your inquiry details are never sold, rented or passed to a third party for any commercial purpose

Cookies and analytics

This site sets no cookies and runs no advertising or third-party tracking scripts. Site traffic is measured with cookieless, aggregated analytics — page views only, no individual visitor profiles and no cross-site tracking.

How long it is kept

Inquiry records are kept while the inquiry is active and for up to 24 months afterwards, so that a repeat order can be handled without you having to explain everything again. You can ask me to delete your record at any time and it will be removed.

Third parties involved

The site is hosted on Cloudflare. If you contact me on WhatsApp, that conversation sits on WhatsApp's own platform under their terms. If a transaction runs through an escrow platform such as Upwork, their terms apply to that transaction.

Getting in touch

To ask a question about your data, or to have it deleted, write to iceggup@gmail.com or message me on WhatsApp.
